# 🖥 Agile Sprint Review Template

A clear, practical Sprint Review template designed to help Scrum teams present their work with confidence, clarity, and consistency. This template includes an agenda, multiple slide layouts, demo formats, and guidance on how to communicate Sprint goals, challenges, and outcomes effectively.

**Read the full walkthrough here:**

---

## 📄 What’s Included

- Title slide for team, sprint name, and date  
- Agenda slide for setting expectations  
- Sprint goal(s) overview  
- Completed and in‑progress user stories  
- Sprint challenges  
- Multiple demo slide layouts  
- Action items and learnings  
- Upcoming sprint preview  
- Closing slide  

Each slide includes guidance on how to present the content during a Sprint Review.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of This Template

Sprint Reviews are most effective when the focus stays on the **product**, not the presentation. This template helps teams:

- communicate progress clearly  
- highlight completed work  
- share challenges transparently  
- gather meaningful stakeholder feedback  
- maintain a consistent review structure

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Add your team name, sprint name, and date.  
2. Update the Sprint goals and user stories.  
3. Insert screenshots or videos into the demo slides.  
4. Share challenges and learnings that matter to stakeholders.  
5. Preview the upcoming sprint (tentative until Sprint Planning).  
6. Leave the closing slide up while attendees exit.
